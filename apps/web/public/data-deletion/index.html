Delete your data

You can remove Social Preneur's access to your social media accounts and ask us to delete your data at any time. Choose the option that fits.

Draft for review. Replace the highlighted details before publishing. Then delete this notice.

1Disconnect an account inside Social Preneur

If you use Social Preneur, open the client's Social accounts page and choose Disconnect. We immediately delete the stored access tokens for that account, stop publishing to it and stop collecting its data. Scheduled posts for that account are cancelled.

2Remove Social Preneur from the platform

You can also revoke access from the social platform itself. Menu names change from time to time, so look for the section that lists connected apps, apps and websites, or permissions.

  • Facebook, Instagram or Threads: in your Meta account settings, open the list of connected apps and websites or business integrations, select Social Preneur and remove it. Meta then notifies us, and we delete the data we received from Meta for that account.
  • LinkedIn: in your settings, open the data privacy section, find the list of permitted or connected services, and remove Social Preneur.
  • X: in your settings, open the security and account access section, find connected apps, and revoke access for Social Preneur.
  • Pinterest: in your settings, find the list of apps connected to your account, and remove Social Preneur.

When access is revoked on the platform, publishing and data collection for that account stop, and we delete its stored access tokens.

3Ask us to delete your data

Email support@vturnai.com with the subject Data deletion request and include:

  • your name and the email address you use with Social Preneur, or the social account you connected;
  • the agency or business name, if you know it;
  • what you want deleted: your whole account, a connected social account, or specific content.

We may ask you to confirm your identity. We confirm receipt within 7 days, complete the deletion within 30 days, and email you when it is done.

5Requests sent by Meta

If you remove Social Preneur from your Facebook or Instagram account, Meta sends us a deletion request on your behalf. We process it automatically and give Meta a confirmation code, which you can quote if you contact us about the request.
