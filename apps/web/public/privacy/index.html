Privacy Policy

Social Preneur is a social media management platform operated by VTurnU Digital Solutions LLP. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1Who we are

Social Preneur ("we", "us") is operated by VTurnU Digital Solutions LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in India, with its registered office at 3-127E, BRG Madepalli, Bargur, Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu 635104, India.

We decide how account, security and usage information is processed. When an agency uses Social Preneur to manage social media for its clients, the agency decides which client content is added, and we process that content to provide the service to the agency.

2Who this policy covers

  • Agency team members who use Social Preneur to plan, create and publish content.
  • Client users who are invited to review, approve or view content and reports.
  • Owners and administrators of social media accounts connected to Social Preneur.

3Information we collect

Account information

Name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, and the agency and clients you are assigned to.

Client business information

Brand details added by the agency, such as business description, products, services, target audience, brand colours, approved facts and content rules.

Content

Post ideas, captions, hashtags, creative briefs, images, videos, documents, comments and approval decisions, including earlier versions.

Connected social account information

Only after the account owner or administrator authorizes a connection: account or page name and identifier, profile image, granted permissions, access tokens and publishing results. Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored.

Performance data

Metrics provided by the platforms for connected business accounts and for posts published through Social Preneur, such as reach, impressions, engagement, clicks and follower counts.

Technical and security information

IP address, browser and device type, sign-in times, and an audit log of important actions such as approvals, publishing and permission changes.

4Data from social media platforms

Social Preneur connects to Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Threads (Meta), LinkedIn Pages, Pinterest and X only through their official APIs, and only after authorization. We use platform data only to:

  • show the pages and accounts you manage, so each can be assigned to the correct client;
  • publish content that has been reviewed and scheduled in Social Preneur;
  • retrieve performance data for connected accounts and published posts;
  • detect disconnected accounts, expired access and publishing errors.

We do not read private messages, we do not access personal profile feeds, we do not sell platform data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles or share it with data brokers. Our use of platform data also follows each platform's developer terms and policies, including the Meta Platform Terms, the LinkedIn API Terms of Use, the Pinterest developer guidelines and the X Developer Agreement.

5How we use information

  • To provide the service: content calendars, suggestions, approvals, scheduling and publishing.
  • To keep each client's information separate and enforce roles and permissions.
  • To secure the service, prevent misuse and investigate problems.
  • To contact you about your account, invitations, approvals and important changes.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

6AI-assisted features

Some features, such as post ideas, captions, platform versions and creative briefs, send relevant brand information and content to an AI service provider to generate suggestions. We send only the information needed for each task. Our AI provider processes this data under API terms under which, according to the provider, submitted data is not used to train its models by default.

AI output is only a suggestion. People review and approve content before it is published.

8Storage and security

Data is stored with our service providers, which may process it in India or in other countries. We protect data with encryption in transit, encryption of social access tokens at rest, role-based access controls, separated client workspaces and audit logging. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the authorities as required by law.

9How long we keep information

  • Account information: while your account is active, then deleted or anonymized within 90 days of account closure, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Access tokens: deleted as soon as an account is disconnected, access is revoked or a deletion request is completed.
  • Content and performance data: kept while the agency uses Social Preneur for that client, then deleted within 90 days of the client or agency account being removed.
  • Backups: overwritten on a rolling cycle of up to 35 days.
  • Security and audit logs: kept for up to 3 years to investigate misuse and meet legal obligations.

10Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask us to:

  • give you information about the personal data we process about you;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it;
  • stop processing that is based on your consent;
  • let a person you nominate exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To make a request, email support@vturnai.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

11Deleting your data

You can disconnect accounts and request deletion at any time. See how to delete your data.

12Children

Social Preneur is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. For significant changes, we will also tell account users by email or in the app.
